### Investor Services

CBK provides services to investors in government securities and the public.

### CDS Accounts
- Required for investing in T‑bills and T‑bonds.
- Opened at CBK or through commercial banks.
- Requires ID, KRA PIN, passport photo, and completed form.

### Internet Banking for Banks
- CBK provides internet banking registration for commercial banks.
- Used for forex transactions, government securities, and OMO.

### Commemorative Coins
- CBK issues commemorative coins for special occasions.
- Available for purchase by individuals and institutions.

### Publications
- CBK publishes circulars, guidelines, press releases, annual reports, and financial stability reports.

### Common FAQs
- **How do I open a CDS account?** → Submit forms and documents to CBK or your bank.
- **How do I buy commemorative coins?** → Complete request forms and pay at CBK.
- **Where can I find CBK circulars?** → On the CBK website.
