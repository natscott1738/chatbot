### Currency Issuance and Management

CBK is the sole issuer of the Kenya Shilling (KES).

### Banknotes and Coins
- Denominations: 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 shillings.
- Coins: 1, 5, 10, 20, 40 shillings.
- Commemorative coins issued for national milestones (e.g., Kenya@50, CBK@50).

### Currency Centres
- CBK operates currency centres across Kenya for distribution and withdrawal.
- Handles withdrawal of old notes and replacement with new issues.

### Anti‑Counterfeiting
- Banknotes include security features: watermarks, holograms, raised print.
- CBK educates the public on how to identify genuine currency.

### Common FAQs
- **Who issues the Kenya Shilling?** → CBK.
- **What are commemorative coins?** → Special coins marking national milestones.
- **How do I exchange old notes?** → At CBK branches and authorized banks.
