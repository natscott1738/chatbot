### Prudential Guidelines and Supervision

CBK issues Prudential Guidelines to ensure stability and soundness of financial institutions.

### Key Areas
- Capital adequacy requirements.
- Liquidity management.
- Corporate governance standards.
- Risk management frameworks.
- Credit classification and provisioning.
- Disclosure and transparency requirements.

### Compliance
- Institutions must submit regular returns to CBK.
- CBK conducts inspections and stress tests.
- Non‑compliance may result in sanctions.

### Related Institutions
- Financial Reporting Centre (FRC) for AML/CTF compliance.
- Credit Reference Bureaus (CRBs) for credit information sharing.

### Common FAQs
- **What are Prudential Guidelines?** → CBK rules on capital, liquidity, governance, and risk.
- **What is capital adequacy?** → The minimum capital banks must hold relative to risk‑weighted assets.
- **What is liquidity ratio?** → The proportion of liquid assets to short‑term liabilities.
