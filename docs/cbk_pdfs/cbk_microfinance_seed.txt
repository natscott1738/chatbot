### Microfinance Institutions and Licensing

CBK regulates and supervises deposit‑taking microfinance institutions (DTMs) under the Microfinance Act.

### Licensing Process
- Submit Licence Application Form.
- Provide details of shareholding, directors, officers, and capital adequacy.
- Complete Fit and Proper forms for directors and significant shareholders.
- Submit audited financial statements, business plans, governance structures.
- Pay application fees and submit to CBK’s Banking Supervision Department.

### Prudential Requirements
- Minimum capital requirements for community and nationwide microfinance banks.
- Liquidity ratios, capital adequacy ratios, and governance standards.
- Compliance with Prudential Guidelines and AML/CTF laws.

### Supervision
- CBK conducts onsite and offsite inspections.
- Institutions must submit periodic returns.
- Non‑compliance may lead to penalties, licence suspension, or revocation.

### Common FAQs
- **How do I apply for a microfinance licence?** → Complete forms, meet capital requirements, submit to CBK.
- **What is a Fit and Proper form?** → A CBK form assessing suitability of directors and shareholders.
- **What is the difference between community and nationwide microfinance banks?** → Community banks operate within one county; nationwide banks operate countrywide.
