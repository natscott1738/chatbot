### Legal and Institutional Framework

CBK operates under several laws:
- Central Bank of Kenya Act.
- Banking Act.
- Microfinance Act.
- National Payment System Act.
- Proceeds of Crime and Anti‑Money Laundering Act.

### Related Regulators
- National Treasury.
- Capital Markets Authority (CMA).
- Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA).
- Sacco Societies Regulatory Authority (SASRA).
- Financial Reporting Centre (FRC).

### Compliance
- Institutions must comply with AML/CTF laws.
- CBK collaborates with other regulators to ensure financial stability.

### Common FAQs
- **What law establishes CBK?** → The CBK Act, 1966.
- **Who does CBK work with?** → Treasury, CMA, IRA, SASRA, FRC.
- **What is AML?** → Anti‑Money Laundering regulations to prevent illicit finance.
